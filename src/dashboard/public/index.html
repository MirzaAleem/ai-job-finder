    Everything here is written to your .env file, with its comments left intact. You can still edit that file by hand — this page is just a friendlier way in.

    Your profile

    This is what every job is matched against. Missing information never rejects a job: a posting that does not state a salary is not treated as low-paying.

    Editing the YAML directly keeps your comments. Saving from the form above rewrites the file and drops them.
